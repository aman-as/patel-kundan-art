What people come to us for

Remaking old gold

Jewellery that sits in a locker because nobody wears it any more — a set from a wedding a generation ago, pieces that came from two sides of a family, something broken or out of fashion.

We melt nothing without telling you what you have first. The old piece is weighed in front of you, and what comes back is weighed the same way.

Ask about remaking old gold

Made to your design

A bridal set, a single piece for an occasion, or a copy of something you saw and could not find anywhere. Send a photograph — most conversations here start with one.

Kundan and jadau is what we are known for, but we manufacture across gold jewellery. If a design is beyond what we do well, we will tell you rather than take the order.

Honest expectations

Everything is made to order by hand, so it takes time — this is not a shop where you choose a piece and take it home the same day. We will tell you the realistic timeline for your piece before you commit to it, and we would rather lose the order than promise a date we cannot keep.

We do not quote rates on a website. What a piece costs depends on what it is. Send us the design and ask.
