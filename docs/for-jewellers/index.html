What a retailer is actually deciding

Four things you want settled before you hand over gold.

  • Your design stays yours

    What you give us is made for you. We do not resell a retailer's design to the next shop that walks in.

  • Gold in, gold out

    Every job is weighed in and weighed out against a written register. You get an account of the metal, not an assurance about it.

  • One bench, not a middleman

    The work is done in our own workshop in Karol Bagh. When you call, you are speaking to the people setting the stones.

  • Made to order only

    We have no shop, no window and no stock on display. We are not selling to the customer you are selling to.

The piece your customer can still say yes to

Weight is what kills the sale at these metal prices. Kundan and polki give a piece visual size without the gold weight behind it — the set still looks like a set, and the bill is one your customer can pay. Lightweight is where the demand has gone, and it happens to be the work we specialise in.

What we can take on

Kundan and jadau setting is our specialisation. Beyond that we manufacture across gold jewellery — bridal sets, chokers, rani haar, bangles, earrings, pendants and rings, in antique and polished finishes. Gold is the bulk of what leaves this bench; silver we take on properly — the setting work is the same work.

Work can start from your design, a photograph, a sample piece, or a rough idea we develop with you. Repeat orders and continuing lines are the work we would rather have.

What we will not put in writing on a website

Rates, minimum quantities and delivery times depend on the piece, the season and how much work is on the bench. Anyone who publishes those numbers is guessing, and you would have to renegotiate them anyway. Call us and we will give you a straight answer for your specific job.
