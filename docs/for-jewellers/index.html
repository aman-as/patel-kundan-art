What we do for jewellers

Manufacturing shaped around the job.

  • Custom manufacturing

    Work from your design or reference, with the specifications and finish discussed before the job begins.

  • Kundan and jadau work

    Our specialist area. Share the design and setting requirements so we can discuss the right approach.

  • Gold and silver jewellery

    We take on custom manufacturing across gold and silver jewellery, to the design and the specifications agreed.

  • Repeat styles and continuing requirements

    If you need a style made again or want to discuss an ongoing line, share the reference and what you expect to need. Timing and capacity are discussed for each job.

  • Job work with your material

    Where you supply the gold or other materials, the intake, recording, work and handover are discussed so both sides know exactly how the job will run.

What matters to a jeweller

A workshop relationship needs more than workmanship.

  • Clear job discussion

    We make clear the piece, the design reference, the materials, the finish, the estimate and the expected timeline before confirming the work.

  • Material recording

    Gold you supply is weighed and recorded at intake. At handover, it is reviewed against the job record and the agreed terms.

  • Respect for your customer relationship

    You are building a business and serving your own customers. We talk to you about the job, and keep your designs and references private.

  • Direct access to the workshop

    You discuss the requirements with the people who coordinate the orders, the kundan setting and the finishing.

  • One-off jobs and repeat requirements

    We can discuss a single manufacturing job or a possible repeat requirement. Quantity, rate and timeline depend on the work and the capacity available.

What we can make

Kundan is the speciality. The brief can be broader.

We specialise in kundan and jadau, and also take on custom gold and silver jewellery. Work may include bridal pieces, chokers, long necklaces, earrings, pendants, rings, bangles, antique-look finishes and other styles, depending on the design and the specifications.

We can start from a drawing, a reference photograph, a sample piece or a discussion of what your customer needs. The list is not complete; send us the actual brief.

With gold where it is, every gram counts. Kundan and polki give a piece a rich, full look without the gold weight, so the set still looks like a set, at a price your customer can say yes to.

Getting a quote

Send us the details. We will discuss the job.

For a useful first discussion, share whatever you have:

  • A design image, sketch, photograph or sample
  • The type of jewellery and the finish you want
  • Gold, silver or another specified material
  • Approximate weight or budget, if you know it
  • Quantity, or whether the job may repeat
  • The delivery date you have in mind

You do not need every answer ready. We can fill in the missing details as we talk. Rates, minimum quantities where they apply, and delivery times depend on the job, its complexity and the work in hand, so we quote for your requirement rather than publishing one rate for everything.

Questions people ask

Do you publish fixed rates?

We give estimates job by job, because the cost depends on the design, material, weight, finish and work required. Share a reference for a discussion about your job.

Do you accept repeat manufacturing orders?

Yes, you can contact us to discuss repeat styles or ongoing requirements. Quantities, rates and timelines depend on the specifications and the capacity available.

Have a job to discuss?

Send your design or requirements. We will discuss the work involved, what we need for an estimate, and the next steps.
