Your first message

What should you share in your first message?

A few details are enough to start the conversation:

  • What you would like made, repaired or remade
  • A photo, sketch or design reference, if you have one
  • Whether it is an individual order or trade / job work
  • Any known material, approximate weight, quantity or target date

You do not need to prepare a formal specification before contacting us.

Questions people ask

Do you only make kundan jewellery?

No. Kundan and jadau are our specialities, but we also take on custom gold and silver jewellery beyond these styles. Share your design or reference so we can discuss the work involved.

Do you work with individual customers as well as jewellers?

Yes. We discuss individual custom orders, and manufacturing and job work for retail jewellers and jewellery businesses.

Can you make silver jewellery too?

Yes, we take on custom silver jewellery as well as gold. The design and the work required decide the approach and the estimate.
