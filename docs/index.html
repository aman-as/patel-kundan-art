Why kundan, now

Your customer still wants a set that looks like a set.

With gold where it is, weight is what kills the sale. Kundan and polki give a piece visual size without the gold weight behind it — which is what a customer can still say yes to this season.

It is the reason this is the work we chose to be good at.

How a job runs

From a photograph on WhatsApp to a finished piece.

01 / 06

  1. 01

    Design

    You send a photograph, a drawing or a piece to copy.

  2. 02

    Weight

    We tell you what the piece needs before anything begins.

  3. 03

    Gold in

    Received, weighed and entered in the job register.

  4. 04

    Ghaat

    The gold framework is built by hand.

  5. 05

    Kundan setting

    The specialist stage, and the one we are known for.

  6. 06

    Finish

    Polished, weighed out, delivered against the same register.
