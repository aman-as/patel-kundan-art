What we do

One workshop. Different ideas. Jewellery made to order.

Kundan and jadau are our speciality, but our work is not limited to one style. We take on custom gold and silver jewellery and discuss each design on its own terms, whether you are creating one meaningful piece for yourself or sourcing manufacturing for your jewellery business.

We start by understanding what you want made, the design or reference you have, and the materials and finish you need. Before work begins, we discuss the process, the estimate and the next steps with you.

Our speciality, and more

Rooted in kundan. Open to your design.

Kundan setting asks for patience, a steady hand and close attention to how the stone, its setting and the finished piece work together. It is the craft at the heart of Patel Kundan Art.

We also work on other custom gold and silver jewellery. Share a reference, sketch, sample or description, and we can discuss how to approach it. The right process depends on the design and the materials involved.

How a job runs

Six steps from your idea to the finished piece.

01/ 06

  1. 01

    Your design

    A sketch, a picture, a piece you love, or just an idea. We start from whatever you have.

  2. 02

    Weight and estimate

    Before any work begins, we tell you what the piece will weigh and what the work involves.

  3. 03

    Gold received

    If you give us gold, it is weighed in front of you and written into the job register. You can also pay for the gold and the making together.

  4. 04

    Ghaat

    The gold framework is built by hand.

  5. 05

    Specialist Craftsmanship & Setting

    The making, stone-setting and finishing processes are selected according to the jewellery design and materials.

  6. 06

    Finish and weigh out

    Polished, checked and weighed out against the same register entry, then handed back to you.

Trust is part of the work

Clear about the work. Careful with what matters.

  • Materials are handled with care

    When gold is handed over for a job, it is weighed and recorded in our job register. At handover, the finished work and the gold are accounted for against that record.

  • The work is discussed before it begins

    We talk through the design, the work involved and the estimate before you decide to go ahead.

  • A direct conversation with the workshop

    You speak directly with the people who discuss the orders, set the kundan and finish the pieces.

  • Made for the requirement

    A single personal piece and a jeweller's repeat requirement are different jobs. We discuss each one according to what it needs.
