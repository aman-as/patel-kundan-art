How a job runs

From a photograph on WhatsApp to a finished piece.

  1. 01

    Design

    You send a photograph, a drawing or a piece to copy.

  2. 02

    Weight

    We tell you what the piece needs before anything begins.

  3. 03

    Gold in

    Received, weighed and entered in the job register.

  4. 04

    Ghaat

    The gold framework is built by hand.

  5. 05

    Kundan setting

    The specialist stage, and the one we are known for.

  6. 06

    Finish

    Polished, weighed out, delivered against the same register.
